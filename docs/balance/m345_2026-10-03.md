# Redwood Coast, Frost Peaks and the Station: measured income and costs (2026-10-03)

**Verdict: with measured income, a casual player clears Redwood Coast in about 53 min and Frost
Peaks in about 63 min, the longest single wait is 4.5 min (the Lighthouse), and each gateway is
affordable 0.9-1.4 min after the previous landmark (my calc, model below).** Valleys 1-3 are
unchanged: the same script reproduces their current costs exactly.

## 1. How it was measured

`econ_probe.gd` (the probe from `rebalance_2026-10-01.md` section 7, extended to valleys 4-5 and to
the Harbor Market / Ski Lodge tills) in a scratch copy of the project with its own user dir.
Godot 4.6.2 headless, `--fixed-fps 60`. The save owns every pad of the earlier valleys (their
landmarks built) plus the first `n` pads of the probed valley; global upgrades at max, region
upgrades 0 unless noted; houses pre-filled; the probed landmark site is left empty; the player is
parked at the valley's handcar stop. Warm-up 120 s then a 180 s window (180 s + 300 s from the
first slipway on, because a ship takes about 2 min). Income = money booked to that valley
(cashier runs, launches, cargo orders, exports) plus coins waiting at its till. Automation only,
no hand work. 28 runs, 7 in parallel. The code measured is the branch as of 2026-10-03 after the
shopper-patience fix (section 4).

## 4. Found while measuring (fixed)

- **Market deadlock:** shoppers queued at a sold-out counter until the market's 20-shopper cap was
  all waiting for deckboards, and the full timber counter never sold again (3 of 4 debug runs at
  pad 12 stalled within 2 min). Shoppers now give up after 15 s at an empty counter (they pay for
  what they already hold). This is shared Shop code; it only acts when a counter stays empty.
- **Haulers holding a load nobody wants:** a routed hauler (forklift, shipwright, snowcat) whose
  drop filled up on the way now takes the load back to its source pile.
- **Masts starving behind timber:** shipwrights served the fullest pile first, so three masts
  waited behind piles of timber; Redwood Coast haulers now favour the emptier slot.
- **No sleds were ever made:** both kilns were belted to the ski workshop, so the sled workshop got
  no dry lumber. Kiln 2's belt now splits 50/50 to the sled workshop once it stands.
- **Build sites and cargo lines** only used up the top item of their intake pile, which a fast belt
  kept in flight: used-up deckboards piled into a tower inside the ship. They now use up every item
  that has landed (measured income above predates this; it only removes a delay).
- **Design flag:** Frost Peaks earns nothing on its own until the snowcat (pad 13 of 19), and
  Redwood Coast nothing until the forklift (pad 12 of 21). The model covers it with the older
  valleys' income and hand work, but the designer may want a hauler earlier.
